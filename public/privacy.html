Privacy & data

Incased Games uses your account to let you publish a review. Your public player name, rating, review and its date are shown to visitors. Your account email is not published. Sign-in is handled by GitHub and Supabase; this website does not ask for or store your GitHub password.

Bug reports are kept in a separate private inbox. You can report a glitch without an account; an optional contact email is used only to follow up about your report. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information in a report.

The game stores saves and preferences in your browser. Website sign-in uses browser storage to keep your authenticated session. Signing out clears that session on this browser. The website does not add advertising or analytics trackers.

When community services are connected, Supabase processes account and community data. GitHub Pages hosts the public site. Their own service privacy policies also apply.

To request removal of your review, account or report, use the private report form and choose Website. Account deletion requests must be verified before action.
